This is a Learnscape-style explorable. A dispatch order carries real state through a reduced power-system model. Every station performs a real calculation or control step.
How much of it is real
Computed live: load, generation, reserve, frequency response, governor response, simplified ACE, AGC correction, generator ramping, and the frequency trace.
Scaled down: three generators and one tie line instead of a full utility network. Time constants are shortened so the mechanism is visible.
Assumed / modeled: generator sizes, costs, inertia, governor droop, ramp limits, control-area bias, and 1968 SDG&E operating details are representative, not archival plant data.
Historical anchors: the 101 Ash Street headquarters was completed in 1968. The Coronado Bridge was under construction in 1968 and opened in 1969.
Deliberately simplified: the COBOL and FORTRAN parsers recognize a small teaching subset. They are not full language interpreters.
